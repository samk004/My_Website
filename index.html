A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before you find your piece.

Can I order from this website?

This is a storefront demo. You can explore products, save a bag, and download an order enquiry. No payment is collected and no order is placed. For actual availability, visit our Etsy shop.

Are these the actual product photographs and prices?

The collection uses original illustrations and sample prices to demonstrate the design. Confirm actual dimensions, materials, prices and availability with the seller before ordering.

How should I care for brass and wooden pieces?

Follow the care instructions supplied with your particular piece. In general, a soft dry cloth is a gentle starting point; avoid abrasive cleaners and prolonged moisture.
